Who we are

A non-profit built on exposure, mentorship and Ubuntu

Our vision

To create a generation of confident, purpose-driven young leaders by expanding their exposure to opportunities, careers, education, leadership, entrepreneurship and life beyond their immediate environment.

Our mission

We partner with schools, communities, businesses and professionals to expose learners from underserved communities to opportunities that inspire confidence, broaden perspectives and empower informed life choices through experiential learning, mentorship and leadership development.

  • Hope
  • Excellence
  • Integrity
  • Leadership
  • Curiosity
  • Courage
  • Accountability
  • Ubuntu

Founded in Centurion, Gauteng by Yamkela Lelo Sirayi, Kelebogile Mohajane and Alutha Gaushe.

What we do

Seven ways we widen a learner's world

Young people can only aim for futures they know exist. Each focus area puts learners in front of a new possibility.

Career exposure

Introducing learners to careers they have never considered.

Pilot · Doctor · Software engineer · Artisan · AI careers

Educational exposure

Visits to universities, TVET colleges, museums, science centres, Parliament and the Constitutional Court.

Leadership development

Public speaking, communication, teamwork, goal setting, problem solving and emotional intelligence.

Financial literacy

Budgeting, saving, investing, credit, tax and personal finance. Planned as our signature programme.

Entrepreneurship

Employment isn't the only path. Business ideas, pricing, marketing, customer service and funding.

Wellness

With professionals: mental health, self-esteem, healthy relationships, GBV awareness and resilience.

Environmental leadership

Hiking, conservation, clean-up campaigns, recycling and food gardens.

Starting with one school

We are piloting with one partner school and a small group of learners, so we can measure what works before we grow.
